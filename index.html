What it measures

  • Templated filler rate — how much of each reply is boilerplate ("breath hitched", "a shiver ran down", "很高兴为你解答").
  • Opening homogeneity — share of replies starting the same way; the classic "everyone has one voice" smell.
  • Truncation — replies that stop mid-sentence or right at the token ceiling.
  • Speaking for the user — the model writing your character's actions and dialogue for you.
  • Read completeness — how many lines were parseable; we say so instead of silently scoring a broken file.

What we deliberately do not claim

  • We do not detect "avoids your question" or "too agreeable" automatically — on 139 real transcripts those two ran at a false-positive rate higher than the real incidence. They stay a human review step.
  • We never say "fine-tuning will fix this". The report ends with either "settings first" or "a fine-tune is worth testing, here is the protocol".
